ICIMTech 2026 Conference Paper

Consumer Acceptance of WhatsApp-Enabled Conversational Commerce in Indonesia: A UTAUT2 Study

Published at ICIMTech 2026 — Hosted by BINUS University & IEEE Computer Society Chapter.

This study investigates key drivers influencing customer adoption and continued usage of WhatsApp Business as a conversational commerce platform in Indonesia based on the UTAUT2 framework.

  • Key Finding: Habit proved to be the strongest predictor influencing consumer behavioral intention and actual usage.
  • Intention–Behavior Gap: Intention does not solely dictate actual usage in mature markets; daily communication habits dominate.
  • Proposed Architecture: Introduces a 5-layer Conceptual Architecture to efficiently support conversational business ecosystems.
